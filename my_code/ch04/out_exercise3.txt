Research Plan: 'The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy'

1. Objective: Identify and locate the book 'The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy' by Douglas Adams.

2. Research Steps:
    a. Bibliographic Verification:
        - Identify accurate title, author (Douglas Adams), and series information.
        - Determine relevant ISBNs for the primary editions.
    b. Resource Identification:
        - Local Libraries: Utilize WorldCat or local library catalogs to check for physical/ebook availability.
        - Online Retailers: Check major bookstores (e.g., Barnes & Noble, Amazon) for current stock status.
        - Used Book Market: Search platforms like AbeBooks or ThriftBooks for older, collectible, or lower-cost copies.
        - Digital Platforms: Verify availability on e-reader (Kindle, EPUB) and audiobook platforms (Audible).
    c. Procurement Plan:
        - Select the preferred format (e.g., physical copy, audiobook).
        - Verify local availability versus shipping/download times.
        - Initiate purchase or request loan.

3. Final Deliverable:
    - A consolidated list of acquisition options, including retailers and library availability status.
