Research Plan: Finding 'The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy'

1. Bibliographic Verification
   - Confirm author: Douglas Adams.
   - Verify original publication year (1979) and key editions.
   - Identify primary ISBNs for original and popular reprint editions.

2. Resource Identification (Physical)
   - Search major library databases (e.g., WorldCat) for locations of physical copies.
   - Query inventory of reputable online used and new bookstores.
   - Contact specialized sci-fi/fantasy book retailers.

3. Resource Identification (Digital)
   - Search major e-book distribution platforms (e.g., Kindle, Apple Books, Kobo).
   - Check public digital libraries and archives (e.g., Internet Archive, Project Gutenberg - though checking copyright status).
   - Verify presence in major online audiobook repositories (e.g., Audible).

4. Data Collection and Organization
   - Maintain a master spreadsheet of sources, categorized by medium (Physical/Digital).
   - Record ISBNs, edition details, store/library name, link, and price/availability.

5. Analysis and Reporting
   - Synthesize data to determine the most accessible version.
   - Compile a summary report detailing where the book can be found, providing direct links or directions for acquisition.
   - Create a final list of recommended sources based on price, availability, and edition quality.
