Research Plan: Finding 'The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy'

1. Search WorldCat: Locate physical copies in local libraries or institutional collections.
2. Query Open Library: Determine if a digital loan or archive copy is available.
3. Query Google Books: Look for previews, bibliographic details, and links to retailers for purchase.
4. Verify Publisher Websites: Check current print editions and e-book availability through official channels.
5. Note on Project Gutenberg: Confirm that the title is not available due to copyright status, and skip if not found.
6. Consolidate results to list available formats (hardcover, paperback, audiobook, e-book) and acquisition methods (library loan, purchase, borrow).
